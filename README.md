# Fortune in a Pot

One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, 120 characters at most,
written as if the plant on the windowsill were having its future told.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9

## The entry

> Soon the sun will linger on your leaves longer, and your keeper will swear you grew overnight to impress the window.

116 characters. The fortune keeps a prediction's cadence, stays warm,
and is tied to this plant in this window: the sun lingering on its
leaves, the keeper noticing. The joke lands gently, the plant showing
off for the window, with no doom and no care instruction in sight.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it with its
character count. The output is fixed, so every run writes the same
bytes.
